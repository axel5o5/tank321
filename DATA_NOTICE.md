# Code and data permissions are separate

The MIT license applies to this project's original software and documentation. The author has confirmed authority to publish and license the original code and the subsequent code changes.

It does **not** grant additional rights to third-party datasets, provider responses, sportsbook inputs, photographs, logos, quoted publications, or other source material. Public availability and inclusion in a source repository are not blanket relicensing permissions.

The research author separately authorized public release of the selected derived research tables: historical team-game results, aggregate pick-value curves, dated team win-total inputs, and simulation outputs. These are identified in the data-provenance documentation. This authorization is not represented as a blanket MIT grant over underlying third-party material; source-specific notices and terms remain applicable.

Upstream inputs and access paths include NBA game data, Basketball-Reference-derived player/draft tables, and cited market forecasts. Newly collected BALLDONTLIE/independent-reference pilot data are separate research inputs. Their provenance, access conditions and redistribution status must be described individually rather than silently covered by the code license.

Credentials, private briefs, internal model-panel discussions, research notes, raw provider captures and private archives are not part of the public release. New player-game pilot captures and raw play-by-play/player mirrors remain excluded. Reproduction from deposited derived inputs is distinct from rebuilding those inputs from upstream raw data.
