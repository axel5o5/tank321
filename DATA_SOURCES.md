## Dictionary

### Historical games

One row is one regular-season game. `gameId` is the unique NBA game identifier (stored numerically, so leading zeros are not retained); `home`/`away` use the 30 team abbreviations in `sim.TEAMS`; `scoreHome`/`scoreAway` are final points; `date` is the game-start date in America/New_York; `home_win` is 1 if the home team won and 0 otherwise. No missing values are expected.

### Aggregate pick values

The CSV has two column-header rows (`curve`, `statistic`) and the index name `overall_pick`. `overall_pick` ranges from 1 through 60. Under each curve, `mean`, `count`, `std` and `smooth` are the empirical mean, number of draft picks, sample standard deviation and weighted isotonic mean.

- `ws4`: Win Shares over the first four post-draft NBA seasons, drafts 1995–2022; unplayed seasons count as zero.
- `ws_car`: career Win Shares through the recorded 2025–26 player-data cutoff, drafts 1995–2016.
- `star`: fraction of draft picks with at least one All-NBA selection, drafts 1995–2018; values are proportions, not Win Shares.

Reading deposited aggregate curves reproduces reported model inputs. It does not independently verify player matching, historical coverage or raw source revisions.

### Market inputs

`team` uniquely identifies a team. `betmgm` and `kalshi` are the original projected regular-season win totals (wins), not fitted season means or observed outcomes. `betmgm_as_of` is `2026-09-23`; `kalshi_as_of` is `2026-07-29`, denoting the dates recorded in the source script. The model uses the identical constants in `WIN_TOTALS`; the exported CSV makes those inputs explicit and testable. Model rating calibration rescales total league wins to 1,230; the deposit preserves the original unscaled market inputs.

### Replay and prospective state outputs

`team` is the team code. A historical key is (`team`, `season`); a prospective state key is (`team`, `state`) within a market-source file. `season` uses start/end years; `state` is a zero-based synthetic-state index, not a date or game ID.

| Columns | Meaning and units |
|---|---|
| `W0`, `exp_W` | Wins at the observed/synthetic break; expected final regular-season wins. |
| `dV_old`, `dV_new` | Expected retained draft-value gain from losing rather than winning one remaining game, under the respective lottery. Units: curve value per game (Win Shares for ws4/ws_car; All-NBA rate for star). Negative values mean losing decreases modeled retained value. |
| `EV_old`, `EV_new` | Baseline expected retained draft value under each lottery, in curve-value units. |
| `dPlayoff`, `dPlayin` | Probability gain from winning rather than losing the paired game, for playoff/play-in participation; fractions, not percentage points. |
| `P_playoff` | Baseline modeled playoff qualification probability. |
| `P_rel` | Probability of the bottom-three/relegated lottery group. |
| `P_np3` | Probability of the other seven non-play-in lottery group (three balls each). |
| `P_pi910`, `P_pi78` | Probability of the 9/10 play-in seed lottery group and the 7-vs-8 play-in loser group, respectively. These group probabilities follow the simulator's lottery eligibility rules. |
| `pre_G`, `post_G` | Historical regular-season games before/after the break. |
| `post_W_actual`, `post_W_expected` | Observed wins after the break; model-projected wins after the break (`exp_W-W0`). |
| `post_shortfall` | Expected minus actual post-break wins; positive means fewer actual wins than projected. |
| `post_winpct` | Actual post-break wins divided by post-break games. |
| `owns_pick` | Historical obligation kind: `own`, `owed`, `protected`, `rollover`, `lesser` or `more`, as defined in `ownership.py`. |

Prospective team means retain `exp_W`, `P_playoff`, `P_rel`, `P_np3`, `P_pi910`, `P_pi78`, `dV_new`, `dV_old` and `dPlayoff`, averaged over states. `win_total` is the unscaled market input; `owns_2027_pick` is a readable selected-obligation label (`yes`, `no`, protected/rollover/linked-rights description).

### Table 1 and conditional Monte Carlo summaries

Table 1 retains `team`, `win_total`, `owns_2027_pick`, `P_playoff`, `P_rel`, `dV_new`, `dV_old` and `dV_new_kalshi` (the Kalshi-source estimate for the same team). Its cohort is the six highest BetMGM new-rule incentives among teams with P(playoffs)<0.9 plus full owners in the BetMGM bottom-eight market-total cohort. Cutoff ties in bottom-eight selection are resolved alphabetically by team code. Every displayed team fully owns its pick under the selected map; this is not the all-picks-owned sensitivity run.

`conditional_state_mcse.csv`: `source`, `team`, `states` (150), `mean_dV_new`, `conditional_state_mcse` (sample standard deviation across states divided by sqrt(150)). `conditional_rank_mcse.csv`: `source`, `team_a`, `team_b`, `mean_difference` (a minus b), `conditional_paired_mcse` (the same SE applied to state-paired differences for adjacent teams among each source's top six point estimates).

These SEs condition on fixed lottery caches, specified market values, one synthetic schedule and other model choices. They are not confidence intervals for real-world behavior and omit shared-cache, source and structural uncertainty. Top point estimates should not be interpreted as confidently separated ranks.
